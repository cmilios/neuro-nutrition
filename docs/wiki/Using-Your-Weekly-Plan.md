# Using Your Weekly Plan

_For signed-in NeuroNutrition users. This page explains how to use the Current
Weekly Plan, inspect Meal Slots, track ingredients and milestones, and request a
Meal Reroll._

[Return to the user-guide Home](https://github.com/cmilios/neuro-nutrition/wiki) ·
[Review your week](https://github.com/cmilios/neuro-nutrition/wiki/Reviewing-Your-Week) ·
[Learn about Start Over](https://github.com/cmilios/neuro-nutrition/wiki/Start-Over)

## Find your way around the Current Weekly Plan

Your Current Weekly Plan is the seven-day plan saved to your signed-in account.
It is the plan NeuroNutrition serves across sessions and devices after checking
the authoritative saved version.

Choose a day to see its four Meal Slots: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack.
The summary above the cards and the calories, protein, carbohydrate, and fat
figures are planning estimates, not medical advice.

## Open a Meal Slot

Select a meal card to open its details. The detail view shows its description,
preparation and cooking time, nutrient estimates, full ingredient list, and
preparation steps when available.

Select an ingredient to mark it checked or unchecked. Wait while **Saving** is
announced; the control is temporarily unavailable until the change is
confirmed. Ingredient progress belongs to the Current Weekly Plan and follows
that plan across supported sessions and devices.

If an older meal has no preparation steps, its detail view says so. You can use
a Meal Reroll to request a replacement with complete details.

## Request a Meal Reroll

A Meal Reroll replaces one selected Meal Slot with a different meal of the same
meal type. It does not replace the rest of the Current Weekly Plan.

1. Find the day and Meal Slot you want to change.
2. Select the circular-arrow control labelled **Reroll this meal**.
3. Wait while that Meal Slot is busy. Do not request another replacement for
   the same slot from a second tab or device.
4. When the operation succeeds, the replacement becomes part of the Current
   Weekly Plan everywhere the account is used.

If the app offers **Try Again**, use that control once. If it says the plan is
checking, out of date, read-only, or still being generated, wait for the check
to finish or use the offered **Reload** control. Do not repeat an action while
its outcome is still unknown.

## Record milestones

Open the account menu, choose **Health Profile**, then **Milestones**. You can
record a weight, an optional body-fat percentage, and an optional note. The
history lists saved entries and allows you to remove an entry.

Milestones record progress information; they do not turn NeuroNutrition into a
medical service or validate whether a goal is clinically appropriate.

## Choose the right kind of new plan

A [Next Weekly Plan](https://github.com/cmilios/neuro-nutrition/wiki/Reviewing-Your-Week)
uses your Meal Review of the immediately preceding plan to balance retention
and variety.

A **Health Profile Plan Replacement** is different. When you change
plan-relevant Health Profile information, the app can offer **Create a new
Weekly Plan from these changes**. That replacement is based on the updated
Health Profile, does not apply Meal Review retention rules, and replaces the
Current Weekly Plan only after generation succeeds. Until then, the previous
plan remains available but may be read-only. A successfully replaced plan
cannot be restored in the app.

## When an action is slow or fails

- **In progress:** keep the page open when practical and wait. The Current
  Weekly Plan may remain visible but read-only.
- **Checking or out of date:** let the app reconcile the saved plan; use its
  **Reload** control if offered.
- **Retry offered:** use the app's own Retry or Try Again control. It preserves
  the intended action safely.
- **Outcome unknown:** do not issue the same action from another tab, browser,
  or device. Wait for the app to reconcile before deciding what to do next.
