# Getting Started

_For first-time NeuroNutrition users. Follow these steps to create an account,
complete a Health Profile, and generate a first Weekly Plan._

[Return to the user-guide Home](Home.md)

## 1. Open the beta

Go to the [NeuroNutrition beta](https://cmilios.github.io/neuro-nutrition/).
Because this is an early-stage beta, behavior and availability may change.

## 2. Create or access your account

- Choose **Create Account** to register with email and password. If the app asks
  you to confirm your email, use the confirmation message before signing in.
- Choose **Log In** if you already have an account.
- Google or Apple buttons appear only when that sign-in method is currently
  offered. If a method is absent or unavailable, choose another offered method.

Keep passwords, confirmation links, and recovery links private.

## 3. Complete your Health Profile

Enter the requested information and check it carefully:

- age, gender, height, and current weight;
- optional target weight;
- activity level and goal;
- diet preference; and
- optional allergy or dislike notes.

The Apple Health control is an experimental demonstration and does not connect
to a real Apple Health account. Enter and verify your information manually.

## 4. Generate your Weekly Plan

Select **Generate Meal Plan** after reviewing the Health Profile. Generation can
take a little time. Keep the page open until the Current Weekly Plan appears or
the app provides a recoverable error.

The Weekly Plan contains breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack for each day.
Open a meal card to see its ingredients and preparation details.

## 5. Check every meal before using it

AI-Assisted Meal Planning provides general guidance, not medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment. Allergy and dietary-restriction inputs do not guarantee
ingredient safety. Check ingredients, labels, quantities, and suitability for
your needs before cooking or eating. Consult a qualified clinician or dietitian
for medical dietary needs.

## 6. Return later

Sign in with the same account to load its Current Weekly Plan. On a shared
device, use the account menu to log out when you finish.
